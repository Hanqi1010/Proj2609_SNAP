Authorized stores per day

Source: USDA Historical SNAP Retailer Locator (Mississippi, data through 2025-12-31). A store is the set of records at the same address with matching names (8,919 stores). A point is one authorization of a store (9,714 in all): breaks in authorization of 90 days or fewer are bridged, and a store that is authorized again after a longer break appears as a new authorization. The 16 USDA store types are combined into six store groups; dollar stores are Dollar General, Family Dollar and Dollar Tree. An ending is the loss of SNAP authorization, not necessarily a store closure. Stores without valid coordinates inside Mississippi are not shown. County and tract boundaries: TIGER/Line 2025. Shading: RUCA 2020 (USDA ERS) primary codes of the census tract, grouped as metropolitan 1–3, micropolitan 4–6, small town 7–9, rural 10. University of Mississippi Medical Center.
